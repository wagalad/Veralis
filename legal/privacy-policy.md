# Veralis — Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** July 2, 2026

Veralis ("Veralis", "we", "us", or "our") operates the Veralis web app (the
"Service"), a scouting tool for VEX Robotics teams. This Privacy Policy explains
what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

**Operator:** Raghav K
**Contact:** orbitvrc@gmail.com

Veralis is intended for users **13 years of age and older**. See the
**"Children"** section below.

---

## 1. Information we collect

We deliberately collect as little as possible.

**Information you provide directly:**
- **Account details:** your email address and a password (passwords are stored
  only as a securely hashed value by our authentication provider — we never see
  or store your plaintext password).
- **Display name:** if you sign in with Google or Apple, we import your name from
  that provider to show it to your teammates. You can change or remove it.
- **Scouting entries:** notes and ratings you record *about robots and teams*
  (drivetrain type, intake, scoring, autonomous performance, a reliability
  rating, and an optional short note). This is competition observation data, not
  personal information about people.
- **Optional robot photos:** if you attach a photo to a scouting entry, it is
  stored in a private storage bucket visible only to your team.
- **Team information:** the VEX team number and name you create or join, and
  invite codes used to join a team.

**Information collected automatically:**
- Basic technical data needed to operate the app (e.g., authentication session
  tokens). We do **not** use third-party advertising or behavioral tracking, and
  we do not sell data.

## 2. How we use information

We use the information above only to:
- create and secure your account and sign you in;
- let you and your teammates share scouting observations within your team;
- operate, maintain, and improve the Service; and
- respond to support requests and enforce our Terms of Use.

## 3. How information is shared

- **With your team.** Scouting entries, robot photos, your display name, and your
  team membership are visible to other members of the same Veralis team. **They
  are not visible to any other team** — team data is isolated at the database
  level (row-level security), which is a core design guarantee of Veralis.
- **Service providers (subprocessors).** We rely on a small number of vendors to
  run the Service:
  - **Supabase** — authentication, database, and file storage;
  - **Vercel** — application hosting;
  - **Google** and **Apple** — only if you choose to sign in with them;
  - **VEX / the Robotics Education & Competition Foundation API** — we send only
    public VEX team numbers and event IDs to retrieve public match, ranking, and
    skills data. **No personal or account information is sent to the VEX API.**
- **Legal reasons.** We may disclose information if required by law or to protect
  the safety, rights, or property of users or the public.
- **We do not sell personal information** and do not share it for advertising.

## 4. Data retention

We keep your information for as long as your account is active. When an account
or team is deleted, the associated personal data is deleted or anonymized within
a reasonable period, except where we must retain it to comply with law.

## 5. Your choices and rights

You can:
- view and update your display name in the app;
- request a copy of your personal data; and
- request deletion of your account and associated personal data.

To make any of these requests, contact **orbitvrc@gmail.com**.

Depending on where you live (e.g., California, the EU/UK), you may have
additional rights over your personal data. We honor applicable requests.

## 6. Security

Access to team data is enforced at the database level so that one team cannot see
another team's data. Passwords are hashed by our authentication provider. Robot
photos are stored in a private bucket. No system is perfectly secure, but we take
reasonable measures to protect your information.

## 7. Children

Veralis is intended for users **13 years of age and older**, and is **not
directed to children under 13**. We do not knowingly collect personal information
from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided us with
personal information without appropriate permission, please contact us at
**orbitvrc@gmail.com** and we will review the request and delete the information.

## 8. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy. If we make material changes, we will take
reasonable steps to notify affected users.
