Veralis — the free VEX Robotics scouting app

Veralis is a free, offline-first VEX scouting app for VRC teams. Track every match live, log structured scouting reports on the teams you watch, look up any team's record, skills scores, OpenSkill rating and awards, and get a live match-queue countdown at competitions — all from your phone in the pits.

Why VEX teams scout with Veralis

  • Shared team scouting — every scout's reports sync into one pool in real time
  • Structured robot reports: threat level, scoring, defense, driver skill, autonomous and more
  • Look up any VEX team's match record, skills scores, OpenSkill rating and awards
  • Live match schedule with a countdown telling you exactly when to queue
  • Works offline in loud venues with bad signal — entries sync when you reconnect
  • 100% free for VEX Robotics Competition (VRC) teams
